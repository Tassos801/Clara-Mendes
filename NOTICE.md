# Third-party data and fonts

Used by the "Your Sky" personalised star map.

- **Star positions** — Yale Bright Star Catalogue, 5th revised edition
  (Hoffleit & Warren, 1991). Public domain. JSON conversion by
  aduboisforge (Bright-Star-Catalog-JSON), released into the public domain.
- **Constellation lines** — d3-celestial by Olaf Frohn,
  `data/constellations.lines.json`. BSD-3-Clause. Copyright (c) 2015,
  Olaf Frohn. All rights reserved.
- **Place names, coordinates and time zones** — GeoNames
  (https://www.geonames.org), `cities15000`. Licensed under
  Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- **Ephemeris** — astronomy-engine by Don Cross. MIT License.
- **Typeface** — EB Garamond by Georg Duffner and Octavio Pardo.
  SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see `public/fonts/OFL-EBGaramond.txt`).
- **PDF generation** — pdf-lib and @pdf-lib/fontkit. MIT License.
